Network build · Valor Node

St. Louis datacenter network redesign

Colocation and dedicated-server clients shared one flat subnet and one gateway, with no isolation between customers. I designed the replacement: every client on their own VLAN and subnet, a rebuilt routing edge for AS33755, and a migration plan that moved clients over one at a time without dropping BGP.

  • Segmentation. Per-client VLANs and /29 allocations with dedicated gateways, carried across Juniper EX4550 distribution and Arista 7050SX / 7048T switching.
  • Routing edge. FRRouting on the edge router with full tables from upstream, dual-stack IPv4 / IPv6, and a cutover from 1G to 10G upstream.
  • DDoS response. BGP communities, remotely triggered blackholing (RTBH), and FlowSpec.
  • Customer BGP tunnels. BGP over GRE for customers bringing their own ASN, with ingress filtering by source ASN built from bgpq4 and nftables.
  • Monitoring. LibreNMS polling the router and switches over a WireGuard management tunnel.
  • Migration. Phased cutover, one client at a time, prefixes announced throughout, and a rollback defined for every step.
